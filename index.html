PRIVACY POLICY.

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Information you provide

When you create an account, the service may process your email address, display name and optional Roblox username. If you publish a post or script, its title, description, code and author details may be stored and shown to other visitors.

2. Authentication and storage

Dyserrealm Script uses Supabase for authentication and database services. Information you submit is sent to that service to provide account, post, script and favourites features. Supabase processes data under its own policies and service terms.

3. Public content

Posts and published scripts are intended to be visible to other visitors. Do not publish passwords, private keys, personal information or other confidential material in posts or code.

4. Saved scripts and local tools

Your saved-script list is associated with your account. The Key Lab generates random strings locally in your browser; generated keys are not registered or validated by a server.

5. Cookies and browser storage

Authentication may use browser storage to keep your session active. The site also uses one necessary cookie to remember your cookie choice. Optional page analytics run only after consent. If enabled, the analytics table stores the page path without query parameters, referring site origin, broad device category, browser family, language, a coarse viewport-width bucket and timestamp. It is not designed to store names, account IDs, passwords, full URLs or visitor IP addresses. You can change or withdraw analytics consent with the Cookie settings button. The hosting/database provider may process network details in standard operational logs under its own policies.

6. Security and retention

Reasonable care should be taken to protect account credentials. Data may remain in the service until it is deleted or removed through available account/database controls. This page does not promise a particular retention period.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact thedyserrealm@gmail.com.

This is a basic privacy notice for this community project, not legal advice. Update it if your data practices change.
